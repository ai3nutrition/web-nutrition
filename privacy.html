Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 July 2026

This policy explains what data NutriA (a personal food diary and calorie tracking app) collects, how we use it, and your rights. We keep things simple and honest.

1. Who we are

NutriA ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile application that helps you log your meals, count calories, and understand the nutritional quality of your diet — using AI-powered photo analysis and a personal food diary. We are registered in Ukraine.

This Privacy Policy applies to the NutriA mobile application and any related services.

2. What data we collect

Account data. When you register, we collect your email address and the password you create (stored as a hash — we never see your raw password).

Food photos. When you log a meal, you may upload a photo. These images are processed by our AI to identify food items and estimate nutritional values. Photos are stored securely on our servers.

Usage analytics. We collect anonymised data about how you interact with the app: screens viewed, features used, session duration, and crash reports. This data does not identify you personally.

Device information. Operating system version, device model, and app version — used for technical support and compatibility.

We do not collect your location, payment card details, or any biometric data other than food photos you choose to upload.

3. Why we collect it

  • Account data — to create and manage your account, send important service emails, and provide customer support.
  • Food photos — to power the AI nutrition analysis that is the core feature of the app.
  • Analytics — to understand how the app is used, fix bugs, and improve features. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the product.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us operate NutriA: cloud hosting, AI processing, anonymised analytics, and email delivery. All providers are bound by data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of NutriA or our users.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and food logs — for as long as you have an active account, plus 30 days after deletion.
  • Food photos — retained until you delete them or close your account.
  • Analytics — aggregated, anonymised data may be retained indefinitely.

6. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, and object to processing of your personal data. EU/EEA users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. Contact: privacy@nutria.app — we respond within 30 days.

7. Children

NutriA is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Contact us to remove any such data.

8. Analytics

We use mobile analytics SDKs to collect anonymised usage data. You can opt out of analytics in the app settings at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will notify you via email or a prominent notice in the app before they take effect.
